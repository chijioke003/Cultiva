Climate-Smart Agriculture

Growing Enterprise, Rooting Equity

We equip women farmers across Northern Nigeria with the land access, capital, and skills to grow beyond subsistence. We work at the intersection of agriculture, enterprise, and policy, turning labor into ownership and harvests into income. Through training, savings groups, and advocacy for land and financial inclusion, we help women farmers become agripreneurs. Because the women who feed Northern Nigeria deserve to hold a real stake in what they grow..

Who We Are

Cultiva Women Agripreneurs Initiative is a women led, youth led organization equipping women farmers with the land access, capital, skills, and voice to turn their agricultural labor into lasting enterprise.

  • Climate-smart agriculture
  • Land and resource access
  • Women & Youth Empowerment
