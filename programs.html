Our Programs

🌱

Climate-smart agriculture

We train women farmers in drought-resistant practices, water management, and diversification, given the region's climate exposure.

🌳

Land and resource access

Advocacy and community engagement on women's land rights.

👩‍🌾

Women & Youth Empowerment

Agribusiness training, starter kits, micro-grants, and mentorship for women and young farmers entering the sector.

📦

Agripreneurship and value chains

Training in agribusiness, processing, and value addition (not just production).

💧

Access to finance

Savings groups, linkages to microfinance/agric-focused funds, and financial literacy.

📊

Policy Advocacy

Land tenure reform, gender-responsive agricultural budgets, and inclusive financial policy
